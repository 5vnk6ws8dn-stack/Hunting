Thresholds reflect Easton/IBO published ranges plus South African/Namibian legal bowhunting minimums for African species. Bighorn Sheep and Pronghorn Antelope are thin-skinned, deer-class animals and sit in the Medium Game tier — they do not require 65 ft-lb "toughest game" energy levels. Elk sits in the Large Game tier (42-65 ft-lbs); many hunters target the upper half of that range (55-65 ft-lbs) for margin, but Elk is not classified in the 65+ tier by itself. Sable and Roan are large-bodied, tough antelope commonly held to the same 65 ft-lb large-game floor as Kudu, Zebra, Eland, Gemsbok, and Wildebeest. Cape Buffalo reflects the South African legal minimum (80 lb draw weight, 80 ft-lbs KE, 750 gr minimum arrow weight, two-blade cut-on-contact broadhead required).
Arrow recommendations are based on Easton's current hunting lineup (Axis, FMJ, 6.5mm, Dangerous Game, Easton 5.0) matched by grains-per-inch and penetration design to typical hide/bone toughness for each class. The Easton 5mm FMJ Dangerous Game (250 spine, 17.7 GPI, N-FUSED carbon core with 7075 alloy metal jacket) is Easton's dedicated shaft for Cape Buffalo and other thick-skinned dangerous game — real-world hunters typically build it out to 750-950+ grain finished arrows to meet legal and penetration requirements.
Always verify spine selection with Easton's arrow selector for your specific draw weight and length, and confirm current law and your specific outfitter/PH requirements before a hunt — regulations vary by species, location, and season. Always verify actual arrow speed with a chronograph — calculated IBO speeds are optimistic.
